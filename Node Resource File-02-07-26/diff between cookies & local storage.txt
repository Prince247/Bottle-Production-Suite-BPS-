------------- Difference Between Cookies & Local Storage-----------------------------
 1. what is Cookies? (Small automatic storage)
 Cookies are small data stored by browser, but automatically sent to server every request
📖 Story:

You enter a hotel.
Reception gives you a wristband (cookie)
Every time you go anywhere → security sees it automatically
You don’t need to show it manually.
Special type:
HTTP-only cookie → JS can’t access it (more secure)

2. Local Storage (Manual storage)
👉 Data stored in browser, but NOT sent automatically

📖 Story:
Hotel gives you a card in your pocket
You must take it out and show it yourself
Nobody checks it automatically
👉 Same in browser:
JS (your frontend code) must send it
